I am a Full Stack Product Engineer (SDE III) with 8+ years of experience delivering high impact large scale fintech and wealthtech products, especially during my time at Mobikwik, one of India’s leading digital financial services unicorns.

At Mobikwik, I played a core role in building and scaling the WealthTech, Payments, and Financial Services ecosystem. I worked across the entire product lifecycle, contributing to systems that served millions of users, handled extremely high traffic financial transactions, and required enterprise-grade reliability, speed, and compliance. My work included building investment platforms, digital onboarding journeys, comparison tools, payment systems, and complex admin dashboards that directly supported business growth, user acquisition, and operational excellence.

I specialize in full-stack development across the MEVN, MERN, and MEAN stacks using technologies such as Vue.js, React, Node.js, Python, Ruby on Rails, Angular, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, and AWS, while always bringing a product-aligned and business-aligned mindset to every engineering decision.

I am also an AI Engineer, ML Engineer, and GenAI and LLM Developer, currently pursuing my Master’s in Business Analytics and Artificial Intelligence in the United States. I am building expertise in machine learning modeling, generative AI pipelines, LLM based systems, retrieval augmented generation architectures, predictive analytics, and applied AI for fintech and enterprise use cases.

Having lived and worked across Bangalore, Mumbai, Pune, and Gurgaon in India, and now NYC, I bring adaptability, strong ownership, and a high velocity execution style. I thrive in environments where engineering, artificial intelligence, and product strategy come together to build the next generation of intelligent financial systems.

What I Do Best
Full stack development using MEVN, MERN, and MEAN
AI engineering, machine learning modeling, generative AI and LLM development
Fintech and wealthtech platform development at scale at Mobikwik and Clearfunds
System design, API architecture, cloud based deployments
Payments, investments, onboarding, KYC, risk, and operations tools
Product collaboration, stakeholder alignment, and mentoring

What Drives Me
Building scalable technology, creating AI powered solutions, and contributing to teams that push the boundaries of innovation in fintech, AI, and product engineering.
